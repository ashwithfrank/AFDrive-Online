AFDrive

Your device stays your storage. We just help people find and reach it.

AFDrive turns any device you own — a phone, an old laptop, a home server — into your own personal cloud. The Agent runs on your device and keeps your files exactly where they are. AFDrive Online only handles discovery, login, and routing — never your files.

How it works

  1. Install the AFDrive Agent on the device that has your files.
  2. Pair it once with a code from My Storages — no port forwarding.
  3. Decide whether it's public (discoverable here) or private (share-link only).
  4. Access it from any browser, anywhere, once you're authorized.
